Teaching, Academic Engagement & Applied Development Policy Practice

His academic engagement bridges theoretical frameworks, governance systems, and real-world policy implementation across employment policy , labour market systems , and sustainable development .

His classroom approach reflects:

  • Global comparative perspectives on development policy and labour market systems
